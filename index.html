Group Leader

Dr. Atul Suresh Deshpande

Dr. Deshpande is a Professor at IITH with extensive international research experience from the Max Planck Institute (Germany) and the University of Pittsburgh (USA). His work focuses on the synthesis and structural analysis of novel materials for energy and biomimetic applications.

Core Research Areas

Carbon Nanoparticles

Carbon nanoparticles exhibit unique optical, electrical, and structural properties. Our research investigates their fundamental characteristics, focusing on their synthesis, functionalization, and behavior at the nanoscale.

  • Synthesis and characterization
  • Surface functionalization
  • Photoluminescent properties

Designing Carbon Nanoparticles

Through advanced computational modeling and rational design, we tailor the properties of carbon nanomaterials for specific applications. This involves predicting structural stability and optimizing material performance before synthesis.

  • Computational modeling
  • Rational material design
  • Performance optimization

Research Focus

Nanoparticle Synthesis

Focusing on multicomponent oxides (high entropy oxides) and high entropy alloy nanoparticles, including detailed structural and functional analysis[cite: 20].

Carbon Materials

Generation of nanostructured carbon materials derived from biomass (like Okra and Corn husk) for energy storage applications[cite: 21, 28, 70].

Biomaterials

Biomimetic approaches for superhydrophobic surfaces, coatings, and bone regeneration scaffolds[cite: 22, 76].
